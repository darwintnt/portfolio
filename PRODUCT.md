# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and potential clients visiting Darwuin's personal portfolio. They scan quickly, evaluate project quality and tech stack, then download the CV or reach out. Context: desktop first, often mobile, both ES and EN.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio that showcases who Darwuin is, the projects he builds, the technologies he masters, and how to contact him. Success = a visitor understands the profile in one scroll, sees real craft in the work, and takes action (CV download or contact).

## Brand Personality

Sober, warm-technical, craftsman. Confidence without shouting; personality through typographic discipline, a warm orange accent and hand-authored details (self-hosted fonts, token-recolorized illustrations), never through generic templates.

## Anti-references

- The original "simplona" pale-cyan template look: plain, single-color, no hierarchy.
- Immersive dark-scrim image cards with white text: they clash with the site's light editorial language and can fail contrast on bright images.
- Uniform identical card grids repeated endlessly with no hierarchy or rhythm.
- Generic AI portfolio aesthetics (gradient text, eyebrows on every section, glassmorphism by default).

## Design Principles

1. Typography carries the hierarchy — by size at a single weight (600), never by weight inflation.
2. One warm orange accent on neutral grounds: #F4F4F4 light / #161120 dark; ink hierarchy expressed by opacity of a single ink color.
3. Motion is quiet and meaningful: parallax decor, d-morph, scroll drift — always honoring prefers-reduced-motion.
4. Hand-authored assets only: self-hosted fonts (Quicksand Variable + Share Tech Mono), token-recolorized Haikei blob, geometric SVG decor. No third-party visual fills.
5. WCAG AA contrast is non-negotiable in both themes; verified, not assumed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA verified for both themes (documented per feature).
- prefers-reduced-motion: every animation has a static alternative.
- i18n ES/EN with runtime locale switching; theme toggle with no FOUC (pre-paint script).
